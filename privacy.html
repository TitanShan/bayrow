Privacy Policy

Four Shapes · Bay Row · Last updated 6 September 2026

Four Shapes does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. The game does not send anything anywhere.

Information we collect

None. No name, no email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifier and no advertising ID. We have no servers and no way to receive information from you.

Information the app keeps on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet:

  • Which levels you have solved
  • Your fewest moves and fastest time on each level
  • The last level and chapter you opened
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings
  • Whether the full set of levels has been unlocked

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

On Android the game requests one permission, VIBRATE, for the haptic feedback when a piece lands. It requests no internet permission, no storage permission, no location, and no camera or microphone access.

Third parties

Four Shapes contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social features and no third-party software development kits.

Purchases

If you unlock the full set of levels, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or receive your payment details.

Children

The game is safe for children to play. No information is collected from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Privacy laws in many places give you the right to see, correct or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Deleting the app removes everything the game has saved.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that change ships.
