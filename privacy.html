Privacy Policy — Four Shapes

Bay Row · Last updated 6 September 2026

Four Shapes does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services of any kind. The game does not send anything anywhere.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID, or any other personal information. We have no servers and no way to receive information from you.

Information the app keeps on your device

So that the game remembers where you were, it saves a small amount of information in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have solved
  • your fewest moves and fastest time on each level
  • the last level and chapter you opened
  • whether you have vibration switched on
  • whether the full set of levels has been unlocked

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes all of it permanently.

Permissions

The Android version asks for one permission, VIBRATE, so the game can give a short tap of feedback when a move lands. It grants no access to any of your information and can be switched off inside the game, under Options. The app requests no internet permission, no storage permission, no location and no camera or microphone access.

Third parties

There are none. Four Shapes contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social features and no third-party software development kits. No data about you is shared or sold, because none is gathered in the first place.

Purchases

If you buy the full set of levels, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your payment details — the app is only told whether the purchase succeeded.

Children

Four Shapes is suitable for all ages and is safe for children to play. Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct and delete the personal information a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to disclose or erase. The information the game saves on your device is entirely yours, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
