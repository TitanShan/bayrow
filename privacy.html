Privacy Policy

Four Shapes · Bay Row · Last updated 2 October 2026

Four Shapes does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. The game sends nothing to us, and we have no servers for it to send anything to.

Information we collect

None. No name, no email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos, no device identifier and no advertising ID. We have no servers and no way to receive information from you.

Information the app keeps on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet:

  • Which levels you have solved
  • Your fewest moves and fastest time on each level
  • The last level and chapter you opened
  • Your sound, music and vibration settings
  • Whether the full set of levels has been unlocked

We cannot see any of it. It is not sent to us and there is no copy of it on our side.

Your phone's own backup

If you have your phone's backup turned on — Android's backup to Google Drive, or an iCloud or computer backup on iPhone — that backup includes the game's saved data, the same way it includes other apps'. It is how your progress appears on a new phone.

That backup is between you and Google or Apple. It goes to your own account, under their privacy policies, and we have no access to it and receive nothing from it. You can turn it off in your phone's own backup settings, and the game will then keep everything on the one device.

Permissions

On Android the game requests one permission, VIBRATE, for the haptic feedback when a piece lands. It requests no internet permission for its own use, no storage permission, no location, and no camera or microphone access.

Third parties

Four Shapes contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social features and no third-party software development kits, other than the store billing component described below.

Purchases

The game currently sells one thing: a single $2.99 purchase that unlocks all 164 levels. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

The payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or receive your name, your payment details or your billing address. The store tells the game only whether this account owns the unlock — a yes or a no, and nothing else.

That check is the only moment the game talks to a network, and it talks only to the store. Everything else works in airplane mode.

Children

The game is safe for children to play. No information is collected from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Privacy laws in many places give you the right to see, correct or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Deleting the app removes everything the game has saved on your device; anything inside your own phone backup is managed through Google's or Apple's settings.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that change ships.
