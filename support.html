Support

Four Shapes is made by one person. Write to me directly and I will answer — usually within a couple of days.

bayrow00@gmail.com

It helps if you include:

  • Your phone or tablet, and which version of Android or iOS it runs
  • The chapter and level number, if it is about one level
  • A screenshot, if something looks wrong

The purchase

What do I get, and what does it cost?

The first chapter is free, and so are the first three levels of every chapter after it. One purchase of $2.99 — or the same price in your own currency — unlocks all 164 levels.

It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There are no ads and nothing that runs out.

I paid, and the game is locked again

This normally happens after reinstalling, or on a new phone. You will not be charged twice — the store remembers what you bought, as long as you are signed in with the same account you bought it on.

On iPhone and iPad: play until the unlock screen appears, then tap Restore purchase underneath the buttons.

On Android: it should restore by itself a second or two after you open the game. If it has not, reach any locked level and tap UNLOCK — Google Play knows you already own it and will give it back rather than charge you again.

If neither works, email me the receipt from Google or Apple and I will sort it out.

Can I use it on my other phone?

Yes, at no extra cost, as long as it is signed in to the same Google or Apple account you bought it on. Install the game and the purchase comes with it.

Progress and backups

Will my progress move to a new phone?

On Android, usually yes. If you have Google's backup turned on — most phones do — Android copies the game's saved data to your own Google Drive, and restores it when you set up a new phone with the same account. Solved levels, best scores and settings come back with it.

On iPhone and iPad, the game's data is included in your iCloud or computer backup of the device, and restores with it.

That backup belongs to you, not to me. It goes to your own account, I never see it, and you can turn it off in your phone's own backup settings if you would rather it stayed on the one device.

My progress is gone

Two things wipe it for good:

  • Clearing the app's storage or data in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling with backup switched off, so there is no copy to restore from.

There is no account behind the game and nothing is stored on my side, so there is no copy of your progress for me to send back. Your purchase is safe either way — that is held by the store, not by the game, and restoring it is covered above.

Everything else

The phone does not vibrate

Check three things, in this order:

  • Options — vibration has its own switch in the game.
  • Low Power Mode, on iPhone. It turns haptics off completely, not down. This is the most common cause.
  • Your phone's own system setting for vibration or haptics.

Most tablets have no vibration hardware at all, and neither does the iPhone 6s or anything older.

I am stuck. Is this level actually possible?

Yes. Every level was solved by a program before it shipped, and the number shown as BEST is the fewest moves that solution took — so there is always a line that reaches it.

When a board looks impossible it is usually the one-way rule: check which direction each row is already committed to. Restart costs you nothing.

Can I turn off the timer?

There is nothing to turn off. The clock counts, but it never runs out and nothing is scored against it. It is there to look at after you finish, if you want to.

Will there be more levels?

Yes, more are planned. I will say what they are and how they work when they are ready.
