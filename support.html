Common questions

My progress is gone

Solved levels, best moves and best times are kept on your device and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no copy of your progress for me to restore. Deleting the app deletes it, and a new phone starts empty.

That is the trade for a game with no account and no sign-in, and it is worth knowing before you clear an app's data.

The phone does not vibrate

Check three things, in this order:

  • Options — vibration has its own switch in the game.
  • Low Power Mode, on iPhone. It turns haptics off completely, not down. This is the most common cause.
  • Your phone's own system setting for vibration or haptics.

Most tablets have no vibration hardware at all, and neither does the iPhone 6s or anything older.

I am stuck. Is this level actually possible?

Yes. Every level was solved by a program before it shipped, and the number shown as BEST is the fewest moves that solution took — so there is always a line that reaches it.

When a board looks impossible it is usually the one-way rule: check which direction each row is already committed to. Restart costs you nothing.

Can I turn off the timer?

There is nothing to turn off. The clock counts, but it never runs out and nothing is scored against it. It is there to look at after you finish, if you want to.

Will there be more levels?

Yes. New chapters are free to anyone who has unlocked the game.
